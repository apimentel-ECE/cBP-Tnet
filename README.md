# cBP-Tnet
cBP-Tnet_Continuous Blood Pressure Estimation Multitask Transformer Network with Automatic Photoplethysmogram Feature Extraction

Cite our cBP-Tnet Research Paper:

Abstract—Hypertension is a serious global health hazard that silently harms critical organs and frequently goes unnoticed, emphasizing critical need for continuous, non-invasive arterial blood pressure (ABP) monitoring. Traditional cuff-based methods outputs only episodic readings whilst alternative invasive methods are clinically difficult and hazardous. Related studies have showed promise in determining BP continuously and without the use of cuffs using photoplethysmogram (PPG) only. However, current methods rely on complex manual feature extraction and/or hybrid architectures, resulting in extended training times and inferior performance. This research proposed cBP-Tnet, a novel efficient single-model Multitask Transformer Network with automatic PPG feature extraction. In addition to being a new method with specialized hyperparameters for BP estimation,  cBP-Tnet’s  novelty further lies on its overall architecture algorithm of combining adaptive Kalman filtering, outlier elimination, synchronization, augmentation, and  employing multi-head self-attention with multi-task learning techniques to detect small and shared waveform patterns associated with systolic (SBP) and diastolic (DBP) pressure. It uses MIMIC-II public dataset (202,956 samples from random 500 patients). Experimental results achieves mean absolute errors of 4.32mmHg for SBP and 2.18mmHg for DBP. These findings meet the Association for the Advancement of Medical Instrumentation's international standard (<5mmHg, >85subjects), making it the first time to meet these requirement for both SBP and DBP. Furthermore, it cuts the number of epochs training by 13.67% when compared to recent AI model, making it more efficient. This establishes cBP-Tnet's potential for integration into wearable and home-based healthcare devices, paving the way for more accessible, dependable continuous non-invasive blood pressure monitoring. 

Keywords— Non-invasive continuous cuffless blood pressure estimation, Photoplethysmogram (PPG), Multitask learning Transformer network, Automatic feature extraction, Deep Learning
